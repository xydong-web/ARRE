F2. 2026-11-04 08:00 CST - **26 of the 60 SynGBO runs (`x4nw2gbo`) crashed with `torch.OutOfMemoryError: CUDA out of memory`** on the 11.72 GiB RTX 4070 Ti GPUs of egroup between 2026-11-03 ~21:00 and 2026-11-04 ~06:30,
    after 29-36 h of running and at 54,520-63,520 of 64,000 oracle calls (the surrogate of SynGBO grows with the number of observations; the process itself held 11.4-11.7 GiB). The other 34 runs finished.
    The watchdog logged them as "NEEDS HUMAN" (a CUDA OOM is not in its transient-fault list); nothing was inspected beyond the error logs and the call counters (`calls.tsv` line counts, `state.pkl` timestamps).
    **Deviation from the letter of the failure rule** ("a software crash is re-run once from scratch"): these runs are **resumed from their last checkpoint** (`state.pkl`, written every 1,000 calls; `calls.tsv` is truncated to the checkpoint; a fresh RNG stream per
    resume, i.e. statistically but not bitwise identical, `repo/ext/synga_cg/run_dock.py`) and, to avoid an immediate second OOM at the same history length, **on 24 GB RTX 4090 nodes (partition `mgroup_sp`)**. Reasons, all independent of any result:
    (i) the recorded recipe of the SynGA/SynGBO runs resumes from `state.pkl` by design (the plan allows the watchdog to re-submit lost tasks "with the recorded recipe"); (ii) the development-panel SynGBO comparator (`x4syngbo`, 45 runs) was produced the same way
    (126 attempts: 54 FAILED, 27 CANCELLED, 45 COMPLETED; 35 of 45 units needed 2-7 attempts), so a from-scratch re-run would treat the trial comparator differently from the development comparator; (iii) a from-scratch re-run reaches the same history length and
    would very probably crash again, turning 26 of 60 SynGBO cells into "missing" and leaving the primary contrasts against SynGBO without a comparator. GPU type changes the speed, not the algorithm.
    **Pre-specified sensitivity (descriptive, added now, before any trial result has been opened):** every SynGA/SynGBO unit records its number of resumes (`state.pkl` field `resumes`, plus the job attempts in `sacct`); the primary analysis uses all runs; a sensitivity analysis repeats the
    SynGA/SynGBO contrasts using only units that finished without any resume (SynGBO: 34 of 60 at the time of this entry; SynGA: 56 of 60), and reports the pocket count of each cell.

- **S1-E6-a  retrosynthesis library.**  S3-GFN's soft constraint needs a retrosynthesis environment (`--retro_env stock_hb`).  The Enamine *stock* list of the paper is not available offline; `repo/ext/s3gfn/data/envs/stock_hb` is therefore the repository's own template set `data/templates/hb.txt` (105 templates, sha256 5ddf9be7...) plus the shared Enamine catalog of the other arms (`data/building_blocks/enamine_catalog.smi`, 2,340,642 lines, sha256 ed8bfb62...), in line with the S1 rule "same building-block release".  The positive share of sampled molecules in the pilot was 0.4-0.5.
- **S1-E6-b  self-evaluation disabled.**  `SynthSmilesTrainer.evaluate` (periodic and final) would dock extra samples; it is replaced by a no-op and the top-100 comes from the shared extractor over the call log (`calls.tsv`, first 16,000/32,000/64,000 distinct docked molecules).
- **S1-E6-c  gradient checkpointing.**  Without the `fast_transformers` CUDA kernel the GP-MoLFormer linear attention falls back to a pure-PyTorch cumulative outer product that needs ~25 GB per 64-sequence graph.  The first pilot attempt (job 671697, RTX 4090 24 GB) ended with CUDA out of memory at about step 7; the model's own gradient checkpointing (identical gradients; switched off while sampling so that generation keeps its cache) brought the peak to 4.5 GiB (job 671698).  No hyper-parameter, loss or optimiser is changed.  (A first pilot job, 671421, was cancelled while pending; it never ran.)
- **S1-E6-d  environment.**  `envs/s3gfn` (python 3.10, torch 2.5.1+cu121, rdkit-pypi 2022.9.5 as pinned in the repository's requirements.txt): numpy pinned to 1.26.4 (pandas 1.3.5 binary compatibility; the repository's pyproject also pins it), and omegaconf 2.4.0, botorch 0.16.1 and setuptools<81 installed in addition because the repository imports them but its requirements.txt omits them.  `pip freeze`: `receipts/s3gfn_env_freeze_20261105.txt`.
